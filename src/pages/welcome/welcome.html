Memory Slot is installed

Two things to know before you start.

1. It does nothing until you switch it on

Memory Slot runs on no site at all until you allow it. Open a page you read in another language, click the Memory Slot button in the toolbar, and turn on Enable on this site. Firefox will ask you to confirm access to that site.

On every other site nothing of it loads, and nothing you read there is seen by it. You can switch a site back off from the same button, or from Settings.

2. Double-click a word

On a site you have enabled, double-click any word — or select a phrase with the mouse. The translation appears over the text, and a card opens in a small window you can drag anywhere. Add to deck saves it.

Saved words come back for review on a spaced schedule: soon while they are new, then at growing intervals. A few minutes a day is the whole idea.

What leaves your computer

To translate a word, that word has to be sent to a translation service. Memory Slot uses Google, Microsoft Bing and MyMemory, in that order, falling through to the next when one refuses. The selected text and the language pair are all they receive.

Nothing else is sent anywhere: no browsing history, no page contents, no account, no analytics. Your cards stay in this browser, and the card manager can export them to a file whenever you want a backup.
